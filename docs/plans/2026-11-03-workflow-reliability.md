# Make the delivery workflow reliable

Thirty-three backlog tickets, each with its own scope and acceptance criteria in the tracker, delivered under one epic. The tickets are binding
for what each one does, apart from the changes under Decisions. This plan decides only what they cannot: order, file ownership and how the work
reaches `main`.

## Finish line

- Each ticket is merged to `main` through the merge queue, and `main`'s CI on the last merged commit passes on the Ubuntu job and `cut-release`,
  with the run linked.
- The full local suite passes on macOS with Python 3.11 at that commit.
- V26-3105's real `ready`, `merge`, tracker snapshot and online `doctor` results are recorded on its ticket.
- Every deferral has a follow-up item, and the epic and its items are closed with this evidence.

## Out of scope

- The release and lifecycle tickets (V26-2803, 2804, 2805, 3096, 3097, 3098), which come next as their own delivery.
- The bundled runtime tickets (V26-2383, 2352, 2348, 2335, 2313, 2838).
- The parked tickets (V26-2828, 3080, 2302, 3083, 3070, 3071, 3072).
- Athena's side of V26-2986 and V26-2807: the `github:pr-merge` helper, and titles from `harness:plan`.
- Cutting a release, which waits for the operator's word.

## Decisions

- **Three lanes, not four.** Review rounds and lead commands both live in `run_delivery.py`, so separate lanes for them would only take turns.
  Lane 2 carries both. The four groups stay as the epic's outcomes.
- **Straight to `main`.** The standing binding's base is `main`, so stories land there through the queue with no integration branch. That makes each
  merge real evidence for V26-3105, and leaves no integration change for V26-3107's review to cover in this delivery.
- **This delivery runs on the installed generation.** Its fixes reach the delivery only through a later release, so each lane works around the
  gaps its own tickets name.
- **Partial tickets.** V26-2986 here keeps the merge command's stderr in the runner's stop detail. V26-2807 here adds the optional title to the provider
  contract, the record, the runner and the generic renderer. Each stays open, or gets a follow-up item, for Athena's side.
- **V26-3094.** The queue stops with `source_conflict` naming the path when the two sides list different outputs, which matches `AGENTS.md`'s rule
  that such lists are merged by hand.
- **V26-3110.** `--destination` stays optional, so existing bindings keep working and this stays a minor.
- **V26-3109's stopped-partway limit is fixed on the page, by the operator's ruling.** A delivery whose close journaled `delivery.closed` but didn't
  finish says so and names `lead-run --resume`. The page can't use `closed.json` for this: it sits beside the flow logs rather than in the state the
  renderer reads, and it's written after the final render, so every clean close would read as unfinished. The close records that it finished in
  what the renderer reads, before the final render. Three cases need a test: a close stopped partway, a clean close, and a record closed before
  this existed, which must not read as unfinished. This part touches `run_delivery.py`, so it lands in tier 5 and the ticket closes then.
- **V26-3100.** Add no churn guard, since noise exceeds the margin. Record that on the ticket, and cache the extraction only if it proves safe.
- **Checks before work.** V26-3086 starts by running its one-line command, and closes with no change if it passes in both orders. V26-3104 starts by
  checking its first item against the current brief, which appears to say the opposite.
- **Design choices left to a lane.** V26-3103's carry-over and V26-3104's other two items are proposed to the lead at ticket start, before any code.
- **Bounded flake work.** V26-3092 and the flakes in V26-3109 each get reproduction attempts under comparable load. A failure that won't reproduce is
  recorded as such with what was tried and goes on the binding's `flakes` with its ticket as `ref`, instead of holding the epic.
- **Posture.** Each ticket's own posture applies. Where it names none, `test-first`, and `characterization-first` for V26-2951, 3092 and 3067.

## Sensors and shared resources

- Each ticket's named sensors in its lane, plus `scripts/validate-registry.py`, `tests/test_registry.py` and `scripts/update-provenance.py --check`.
- The queue's selected checks at each merge, and the full macOS run on Python 3.11 before the epic closes.
- One full suite per hand-off head, with four workers at most, and reviewers run affected modules only. Nobody uses `pkill` or `killall`.
- The in-app port is held through its lease. V26-2951 and 3065 change how that lease and its processes behave, so they each test against fixtures
  rather than a real held port.
- A pin proved red refreshes the file's digest with it, so that the failure is the pin's own.
