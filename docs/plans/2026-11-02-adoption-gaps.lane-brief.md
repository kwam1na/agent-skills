**Epic: V26-3053, the workflow does the lead's and lane's mechanical duties itself. These are the lane rules.**

**Plan**

- The approved plan is `docs/plans/2026-11-02-adoption-gaps.md`. Its review evidence is
  `docs/plans/2026-11-02-adoption-gaps.review.json`.
- Read your ticket in Linear, then your unit in the plan ("In scope, by unit"), its required scenarios under
  "Test scenarios", and the contract decisions (C1 to C9) your unit names.
- The plan's contract decisions are binding. Do not redesign them; raise a disagreement with the lead instead.

**Repository**

- Read `AGENTS.md` first. Its rules apply: provenance through `scripts/update-provenance.py`, catalog entries with a
  releasable skill's change, two-space JSON with a final newline, outcome names in durable text.
- Your session has its own worktree. Before your first commit, make sure your branch starts at the delivery base:
  `git fetch -q origin && git reset --hard origin/delivery/adoption-gaps`. Never edit the lead's checkout or the base
  branch, and never merge the base into your branch: the queue syncs it at merge time.
- Your branch name must start with `claude/<ticket in lowercase>-`, such as `claude/v26-3054-setup-refusals`. The
  queue finds your candidate by that prefix on `origin`, so keep exactly one such branch. Rename yours with
  `git branch -m` before the first push if it doesn't match.
- Bootstrap your worktree's own test environment before any sensor: `python3 scripts/bootstrap-test-environment.py`.
- Tests use temporary repositories, remotes and fake servers bound to port 0, and name no real organization,
  repository or ticket. Supervisor tests are `run_serially = True` and stop every process they start.

**The focused lane profile**

1. Run your unit's required scenarios first, written test-first.
2. Then run your unit's sensors only, as the plan lists them, with `.venv/bin/python`, for example
   `.venv/bin/python -m unittest tests.test_run_delivery` and `.venv/bin/python scripts/validate-registry.py`. Never run
   the full suite (`scripts/run-tests.py` or `unittest discover`) in the lane; the queue runs it on the synced head.
3. Review with two lenses as subagents, `lens.outcome-correctness` and `lens.adversarial-testing`. Pass them the
   ticket's contract and the plan unit, and tell them not to spawn subagents. Resume the same reviewers each round
   until both approve, within `$review-work`'s round bound. At the bound, stop and report the blocker to the lead.
   The adversarial lens limits mutation and fault injection to your changed lines and the code that calls them.
4. Run the repository's compounding check: decide with `$compound-learning` whether the unit taught something worth a
   solution note under `docs/solutions/`, and run `.venv/bin/python scripts/update-provenance.py --check`.
5. Push your branch and open a draft change request:
   `gh pr create --draft --repo kwam1na/agent-skills --base delivery/adoption-gaps`, titled `[<ticket>]: <outcome>`,
   with a body of Summary, Why, Validation, then the Linear ticket link.

**Triage of findings**

- Blocking findings are always fixed in the lane.
- Cheap non-blocking findings, about an hour or less, are fixed in the lane with no ticket.
- Expensive non-blocking findings come to the lead.
- A finding first raised in a later round, against code an earlier round approved, is judged on scope before it
  blocks.

**Hand-off**

Hand off through the state tool (below), then send the lead one message containing:

- the candidate reference (your branch) and the draft change request;
- the reviewed head;
- the review rounds;
- the validation: commands and their results;
- the run id: `none`;
- declared lead inputs: `none` (the binding declares none);
- every expensive non-blocking finding, with its lens and why it is expensive, or `none`.

Do not merge, release, mark your change request ready, or hold a lease you were not granted. The lead merges through
the queue after syncing the candidate with its base. After hand-off, do not push to your branch unless the lead
returns the candidate to you.

**Shared files**

- `provenance.lock.json` is regenerated by the queue; refresh it in your branch with the writer, never by hand.
- `catalog.json`: each lane changes only its own skill's entry.
- `skills/run-delivery/scripts/run_delivery.py` and `tests/test_run_delivery.py` are edited by several units. Touch
  only the commands and tests your unit owns. Setup lands before Lead supervisor and Plan review; Lane commands and
  Rounds touch only their own commands.
- `skills/orchestrate-work/references/schemas.json` is edited by Bases first, then by Never-started.
- `skills/run-delivery/references/config.md`: each unit edits only the entries its unit names.
- When two lanes collide in a shared file, the later change request is returned to rebase. Keep each edit to your own
  entries, and regenerate generated files rather than merging them by hand.

**Tracker updates**

Post on your own Linear ticket, with your host's Linear tools:

- at start, and move the ticket to In Progress;
- at each decision you make: what and why;
- at each review round: the lenses' verdicts, the findings and the fixes;
- at each finding you pass to the lead to defer, naming it;
- at hand-off: the change request, the head, the rounds and the validation.

One attempt each, never a gate. With no tracker tools, carry on and say so in your hand-off.



Say what you are doing as it changes, and hand your work off yourself:

```bash
python3 -B skills/orchestrate-work/scripts/delivery_state.py --dir /Users/kwamina/delivery-artifacts/adoption-gaps focus --lane <lane> --text "<what you are doing>" [--state working|reviewing|blocked] [--waiting-on <who>]
```

Hand-offs wait for the lead's acceptance. One attempt, never a gate: when you cannot write to the record, give each
