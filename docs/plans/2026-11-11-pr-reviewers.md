# Wait for, and gate on, every AI review a PR gets

Epic V26-3344. run-delivery waits for exactly one pipeline reviewer. `review.pr` is `{author, waitSeconds}`, and `pr_review_state` in
`skills/run-delivery/scripts/run_delivery.py` keeps the PR comment whose author display name equals `author`. That comment counts as
current when it was updated no earlier than the latest check at the head. An outside adopter's PRs (3478 to 3504) get two AI reviews as
parallel pipeline steps that run with failures ignored, and only one of them is read. What that leaves:
- The second reviewer is never waited for or triaged.
- A lane can defer a finding the gating reviewer blocks on and still be accepted, though the PR can't merge without that reviewer's
  approval.
- Freshness is a timestamp guess. With overlapping pipelines, a review of an older head passes as current.
- A reviewer step that fails leaves the pipeline green, so the wait runs its full `waitSeconds` and stops with nothing to act on.
- One transient forge error ends the wait. The Bitbucket adapter has no retry, and `call_adapter` stops on any non-zero exit.
- Matching by display name breaks on a rename, or matches another bot posting under the same name.

Everything here is in `skills/run-delivery/`: `run_delivery.py` (`pr_review_config`, `pr_review_state`, `pr_review`, `handoff`,
`lead_accept`, `pr_review_text`, the doctor's `review_pr`, and the `pr-review-current` reviewGate), both forge adapters' `reviews`, the
lane-brief template and the references. The adopter's side, reviewers that post Code Insights reports and the adopter's own
configuration, is tracked in the adopter's tracker.

The delivery runs on its own integration branch, `integrate/pr-reviewers`, cut from `main` at `fa1f09f`, so that it doesn't collide
with deliveries already in flight on `main` (V26-3406 among them, whose lane 2 owns the same `skills/run-delivery/` files). It runs in
two lanes that share `run_delivery.py` and the forge adapters by region, under the lead's coordination (Decision 13), since file-level
ownership would put all four items in one lane, one after another. The branch is merged into `main` once those deliveries have landed
(Decision 11).

The items are V26-3345 to V26-3348, already in Linear under the epic. Each story's criteria below are the tracker item's, word for
word. The decisions settle what they leave open, and each story's rows name the tests that prove them. V26-3349, the reviewer re-run
grant, stays in Backlog, outside this plan.

## Decisions

1. **The configuration.** `review.pr` takes either the legacy `{author, waitSeconds}` or
   `{waitSeconds?, settleSeconds?, reviewers: [...]}`. The two are told apart by `reviewers`: a `review.pr` with `reviewers` is the new
   shape, and one without is legacy. The legacy shape is read exactly as today: `author` required, other members ignored, and
   `waitSeconds` read with `int()`. In the new shape, each reviewer is `{id, account?, author?, signature?, required, gate?, report?}`.
   - `id` matches `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]*$` and is unique.
   - A reviewer needs `account` or `author`.
   - `required` is a boolean, and absent means `true`.
   - `gate` is `"approval"` or `"verdict"`. `gate: "approval"` needs `account`.
   - `report` is a non-empty string.
   - `settleSeconds` defaults to 300 and `waitSeconds` to 1800, both non-negative integers.

   Any other member, a duplicate `id`, a bad `id`, an empty list, a negative or non-integer number, or an `"approval"` gate without
   `account` is refused, naming the reviewer and the member. Every reader of `review.pr` goes through one parser, `pr_review_config`, so
   the refusal is the same everywhere. The readers are `pr-review`, `pr-review-current`, `handoff`, `lead-accept`, `lead-brief`'s PR-review
   paragraph and the doctor's `review_pr` row, which reports it as `wrong`.

   The doctor warns, without failing, in two cases:
   - a reviewer has neither `account` nor `signature`, since a display name alone can match another bot;
   - two reviewers share an `account` or an `author` and neither has a `signature`.

   Internally, the legacy shape becomes one required reviewer `{id: "review", author}` with no settle (Decision 4) and its own record and
   text (Decision 7).
2. **Matching.** A comment belongs to a reviewer when its `account` equals the reviewer's `account`, if set, and otherwise when its
   `author` equals the reviewer's `author`. When `signature` is set, the comment's body must also contain it as a plain substring, not a
   pattern. In the new shape, a comment with an empty body never matches. This excludes GitHub's bodiless `APPROVED` reviews, which
   are approvals (Decision 5), not reviews. The legacy shape keeps today's matching, empty bodies included. Among a reviewer's
   matching comments, the latest by `at` is its review, whatever order the adapter lists them in.
3. **Freshness, without a report.** A reviewer's review is current when every check at the head has passed and the review's `at` is no
   earlier than the latest check's `at`, as today. A failed check at the head refuses at once, on every path, report or not, as today.
4. **The poll and the settle.** Each poll classifies every reviewer as `pending`, `current`, `absent` or `silent`.
   - **When the settle runs.** It starts once every check at the head has passed, at the latest `doneAt` among all the head's checks
     (Decision 5), whichever check was created last. A check with no `doneAt` contributes its `at` instead. It ends `settleSeconds` later by the local UTC clock, and the boundary
     belongs to the settle: at exactly `doneAt + settleSeconds` a reviewer is still `pending`.
   - **After the settle.** An optional reviewer with no current review is `absent`, and a required one is `silent` (V26-3347).
   - **Before the settle**, or while any check is pending, a reviewer with no current review is `pending`.
   - **When the wait ends.** It ends when no reviewer is `pending` and no check at the head is pending, or at its deadline.
   - **Without `--wait`.** The settle runs then too: `pr-review` reads once and reports what that read shows, so a first read long
     after the pipeline went green can be `silent` at once.
   - **The legacy shape** has no settle. Its one reviewer is `pending` until current, as today.
   - **The clock.** Time, sleep and the poll interval are read through one injectable clock (Decision 12).
5. **What the adapters return.** `reviews` gains these members:
   - `account` on each comment: Bitbucket's `user.uuid`, GitHub's `author.login`.
   - `doneAt` on each check: Bitbucket's `updated_on` for a status that is no longer `INPROGRESS`, and GitHub's `completedAt`.
   - `approvals`: `[{account, name, at, commit?}]`. Bitbucket's come from participants with `approved: true` (`participated_on`, no
     commit), and GitHub's from reviews in state `APPROVED` (`submittedAt`, `commit.oid`).
   - `reports` (V26-3348, Decision 9).

   A member an adapter doesn't return is absent, which is different from an empty one:
   - **The legacy shape** reads none of them, so it works with an adapter that returns none.
   - **The new shape** refuses when `reviews` returns no member it needs, naming that member. It needs `account` on each comment when a
     reviewer has `account`, `approvals` when a reviewer has `gate: "approval"`, and `reports` when a reviewer has `report`.
   - **A member present but empty**, or comments with `account` that match nobody, is not refused. The reviewer is `pending`, or its
     gate is unmet.
6. **Retries.** An adapter reports a transient failure by exiting 75 (`EX_TEMPFAIL`): a timeout, a connection error, or an HTTP 5xx or
   429. Both reference adapters do so. Any other failure, a 4xx among them, keeps exit 1.
   - **What retries.** `call_adapter` raises a transient refusal for exit 75. Only the calls inside a wait's loop retry it: `reviews` in
     `pr-review --wait`, and `get` and `reviews` in `pr-review-current --wait`. The backoff starts at 5 seconds and doubles to at most 60,
     until the wait's deadline.
   - **What doesn't.** The `find` before each loop, every call without `--wait`, and every other command refuse at once on a transient
     failure.
   - **At the deadline**, the refusal carries the last transient error's message.
   - **The legacy shape** is affected too. The only behaviour it changes is a wait that used to end on a transient error and now keeps
     waiting, which is the fix the epic asks for. With Decision 2's empty-body rule kept off the legacy shape, retrying is the only
     legacy difference.
   - **Docs.** `config.md` documents exit 75 for a `forge.argv` adapter. It also says a reviewGate's `timeoutSeconds` must exceed
     `waitSeconds` by at least 120, so that the last backoff and one adapter call fit, and `adopting.md`'s worked binding moves from
     1900 to 1920 to match.
7. **The record.** In the new shape, `pr-review.json` is `{schema: "pr-review/2", head, url, reviewers: {...}}`. Each entry under a
   reviewer's id is `{state, required, author?, account?, at?, body?, report?, gate?, triage?}`, where `state` is `current` or `absent`,
   and `gate` is `{kind, met, why}`.
   - **The legacy shape** writes the old record, `{head, url, author, at, body}`, and prints exactly as today.
   - **`handoff` and `lead-accept` read both shapes.** An old-shape record is reviewer `review`, checked on the same head, currency and
     unchanged rules as any other, with the same refusals.
   - **The review move.** `pr-review` makes the `pipeline-green` review move once every required reviewer is current and no check is
     pending, as it does today for the one reviewer.
   - **`lead-accept`'s checks.** It re-checks, for every reviewer, required or optional, that what the record calls current is still
     current and unchanged at the head. An optional reviewer recorded `absent` that is now current sends the lane back to read it.
8. **Triage.** `handoff --pr-review-triage` is repeatable.
   - `ID=TEXT` names a reviewer.
   - A bare `TEXT` is accepted only when exactly one review is current, and is that one's. In the legacy shape that is always the case,
     so `--pr-review-triage TEXT` works as today.
   - Every current review needs a triage. An absent reviewer takes none.
   - A triage for an unknown or absent id is refused.

   The hand-off message shows each triage.
9. **Reports (V26-3348).** Bitbucket's `reviews` returns the reports at `--head`, from `/commit/{head}/reports`:
   `[{id: external_id, result, at: updated_on, data: [{title, type, value}]}]`, with `result` one of `PASSED`, `FAILED`, `PENDING`.
   - **GitHub** returns `reports: null`. A reviewer with `report` under the GitHub adapter is refused by the doctor and by every reader,
     naming Bitbucket as the forge that has reports.
   - **When it is current.** With `report` set, a reviewer is `current` when its report exists at the head with result `PASSED`, whatever
     the timestamps say. Decision 3's failed-check refusal still applies, and Decision 4's wait still waits for pending checks.
   - **Its text.** The reviewer's text is its latest matching comment whose `at` is no earlier than the earliest check's `at` at the
     head, which is when the head's pipeline started. So a comment from an earlier head's pipeline never counts, while a comment posted
     before its report turned `PASSED`, as the report contract has it, does. With no such comment yet, the reviewer stays `pending`
     until the settle. After the settle it is refused, saying the report names a review that wasn't posted.
   - **`PENDING`, or no report yet,** is `pending` until the settle, then `silent` or `absent` as in Decision 4.
   - **A `FAILED` report** makes a required reviewer `silent` at once, with the refusal saying the reviewer errored, and an optional one
     `absent` at once.
10. **Gates (V26-3346).** A gate is checked only on a current review. An absent optional reviewer's gate is not checked anywhere.
    - **`"verdict"`.** With a report, the verdict is the report's data item titled `Verdict`, matched case-insensitively. Without one, it
      is the review body's first line matching `^\*\*Verdict:\*\*\s*(.+)$`, and later lines are ignored. Where both exist, the report
      wins. The gate is met when the verdict's first word, lowercased with punctuation stripped, is `approve` or `approved`. Otherwise it
      is unmet, naming the value, or saying that the verdict is missing.
    - **`"approval"` with a report** reads the report's result and verdict instead, as the verdict gate does, since a report is tied to
      the commit.
    - **`"approval"` without a report** is met when an approval's `account` equals the reviewer's `account`. Display names are never
      compared. Where an approval names a `commit`, that commit must be the head. Bitbucket ties no approval to a commit, so there an
      approval of an earlier head counts. `config.md` says so in a sentence, which is criterion 4's "the docs say which applies".

## Stories

### V26-3345. Read several PR reviewers, each required or optional

Lane 1, tier 1, not blocked. Characterization first, then test-first, per Decisions 1 to 8 and 12. The clock comes first. Then
characterization rows pin the legacy configuration's printed text, refusals, record and exit codes for `pr-review`, `handoff`,
`lead-accept` and `pr-review-current`, before any behaviour changes. Files: `skills/run-delivery/scripts/run_delivery.py`, both forge
adapters, `skills/run-delivery/templates/lane-brief.md`, `skills/run-delivery/SKILL.md`, `skills/run-delivery/references/config.md`,
`skills/run-delivery/references/adopting.md`, `tests/test_run_delivery_pr_review.py`, `tests/test_forge_adapters.py`, and the new
module's catalogue entry in `.agents/orchestration.json` and `tests/test_own_deliveries.py`.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Each reviewer is matched by `account` (the forge's account id, which both adapters' `reviews` now return) when set, else by `author`,
   and in either case by `signature` in the body when set. The doctor warns when a reviewer has neither `account` nor `signature`, and
   refuses duplicate `id`s.
2. Each poll classifies every reviewer as `pending`, `current` or (optional only) `absent`: absent once checks passed and no matching
   comment arrived within `settleSeconds` (default 300). The wait ends when no reviewer is pending. A failed check refuses at once, as
   today.
3. `pr-review.json` holds every reviewer's review or `absent`. `pr-review` prints each under its `id`, and makes the review move once
   every required one is current.
4. `handoff --pr-review-triage ID=TEXT` is repeatable and required for each current review; a bare `TEXT` still works when exactly one
   is current.
5. `lead-accept` and `reviewGate` check every required reviewer is current, triaged and unchanged. An optional reviewer recorded absent
   that is now current sends the lane back to read it.
6. A forge call inside the wait is retried with backoff on a timeout, a connection error or a 5xx, until the wait's deadline. A 4xx
   still refuses at once.
7. The legacy config behaves exactly as today. `config.md`, `adopting.md`, `SKILL.md` and the lane-brief text describe the list.

Settled by the decisions:
- `reviewGate` runs in the queue's checkout, which holds no lane record, so it checks what it can see there: every required reviewer is
  current, and its gate, once V26-3346 lands, is met. Triage and "unchanged" are `lead-accept`'s, and the queue merges only accepted
  candidates.
- Until V26-3347, a required reviewer past the settle stays `pending`, as today.
- Criterion 6 is Decision 6, with 429 counted as transient. Criterion 7's "exactly as today" holds apart from Decision 6's retry
  (Decisions 1 and 7).

Rows, each with its allow side beside its deny side:
- **Configuration**, through every reader Decision 1 names: an unknown member, a duplicate id, an empty list, a bad id, a negative
  `settleSeconds`, and `gate: "approval"` without `account`. Each is refused, naming the reviewer and the member. A valid two-reviewer
  configuration passes each reader. A string number for `settleSeconds` or `waitSeconds` in the new shape is refused. A legacy
  configuration with an extra member and a string `waitSeconds` passes as today.
- **Docs**: `adopting.md`'s worked reviewGate binding has `timeoutSeconds` at least `waitSeconds` + 120.
- **Doctor warnings**: a reviewer with neither `account` nor `signature`, and two reviewers sharing an `author` with no `signature`. Each
  is `warn`, and the doctor still exits 0. With an `account` or a `signature`, there is no warning.
- **Matching**:
  - an `account` match where the display name differs;
  - a same-name comment from another account, which doesn't match;
  - a `signature` containing regex metacharacters, matched as text;
  - an empty-bodied comment, which never matches in the new shape and matches as today in the legacy shape;
  - two matching comments, an older one before the latest check and a newer one after it, with the older listed last: `current`,
    recording the newer one's body.
- **Members** (Decision 5):
  - `account` configured, and the adapter returns comments without `account`: refused, naming the member;
  - comments with `account` that match no reviewer: `pending`;
  - a legacy configuration with an adapter that returns none of the new members: unchanged.
- **Classification and the wait**, on the injected clock:
  - two reviewers, one current and the other still pending, before the settle: the wait continues;
  - both current: the wait ends;
  - an optional reviewer with nothing after the settle: `absent`;
  - a pending check with every reviewer current: the wait continues;
  - a failed check: refused at once.
- **The record and its move**: a new-shape record with one current and one absent reviewer. The move is made only once every required
  reviewer is current.
- **Triage**, with two current reviews:
  - a bare TEXT: refused;
  - one `ID=TEXT` only: refused, naming the other;
  - both given: accepted;
  - a triage for an absent or an unknown id: refused.

  With one current review, a bare TEXT is accepted.
- **`lead-accept`**:
  - new shape: current, triaged and unchanged is accepted, and a changed optional review is sent back;
  - an optional reviewer recorded `absent` that the forge now shows current: sent back, naming it;
  - an old-shape record at the head, current: accepted;
  - an old-shape record at an older head: refused with the new shape's reason;
  - an old-shape record whose review the forge shows changed: refused with the new shape's reason.
- **`reviewGate`**: every required reviewer current passes. One required reviewer pending refuses, naming it. An absent optional
  reviewer doesn't block.
- **Retries**:
  - Each adapter exits 75 on a 503, a 429, a timeout and a connection error, and exits 1 on a 401 and a 404.
  - `pr-review` without `--wait`, and `handoff`, refuse at once on an exit-75 adapter.
  - `pr-review --wait` succeeds after two transient failures.

### V26-3348. Read each PR reviewer's Code Insights report at the commit it reviewed

Lane 2, tier 1, after V26-3346 merges; wired in after V26-3347 merges (Decision 13). Test-first, per Decisions 5, 9 and 10.
Files: `skills/run-delivery/scripts/pr_review_gates.py`, `tests/test_run_delivery_pr_review_gates.py`, and, under a moved ownership, the
adapters' `reports`, the classification call site in `pr_review_state`, `tests/test_forge_adapters.py` and `adopting.md`.

Acceptance criteria:
1. A reviewer takes an optional `report` (the report's `external_id`). The Bitbucket adapter's `reviews` returns the reports at `--head`
   (`/commit/{sha}/reports`): external id, result, created/updated time, and its data fields. The GitHub adapter maps check runs the same
   way, or says it can't.
2. With `report` set, the reviewer is current exactly when its report exists at HEAD; the comment is then read only for its text to
   triage. A FAILED report result means the reviewer errored, and stops the wait as the silent-reviewer story does, at once rather than
   after the settle.
3. The report's data carries the verdict; the `"verdict"` gate reads it from there when present.
4. Without `report`, the comment-and-timestamp rule stays as it is.
5. `adopting.md` documents the report contract: external id, result PASSED once the review is posted (whatever its verdict), FAILED when
   the reviewer errored, verdict in data.

Settled by the decisions:
- The GitHub adapter says it can't (Decision 9).
- "Exists" means exists with result `PASSED`. A `PENDING` report is the "visible as in progress" case of V26-3347's criterion 3, and
  stays `pending` until the settle.
- A FAILED report stops the wait for a required reviewer only; an optional one is `absent`.
- A current report with no comment since the head's earliest check's `at` waits until the settle, then refuses.
- V26-3346's criterion 4 gains its report row here.

Rows:
- **The path**: the Bitbucket fake serves a `PASSED` report at another sha, and a different report, or none, at
  `/commit/<head>/reports`. The adapter returns the head's report and requests exactly that path. A run where the PR's reported head
  differs from `--head` reads `--head`'s reports.
- **Report results**:
  - two checks, all passed, and a `PASSED` report with a comment posted between the earliest check's `at` and the latest's: current;
  - `PASSED` with a check still pending: the wait continues;
  - `PASSED` with a failed check: refused at once;
  - `PENDING` before the settle: `pending`; after it, `silent`;
  - `FAILED` on the first read, before the settle: required exits 3, saying the reviewer errored, and optional is `absent`.
- **Report text**:
  - a `PASSED` report whose only matching comment predates the head's earliest check: `pending`; after the settle, refused;
  - a `PASSED` report whose comment was posted before the report turned `PASSED`, within the head's pipeline: current, with that
    comment's text.
- **Verdict from the report**:
  - a data item titled `verdict` in lowercase is read;
  - a report verdict that disagrees with the body's line wins;
  - `"approval"` with a report reads the report.
- **Members**: `reports` present but empty is `pending`. `reports` absent with `report` configured is refused, naming the member. Under
  the GitHub adapter, a reviewer with `report` is refused, naming Bitbucket.
- **Without `report`**: the V26-3345 classification rows pass unchanged.

Sensors: `tests/test_run_delivery_pr_review_gates.py`, `tests/test_run_delivery_pr_review.py`, `tests/test_forge_adapters.py`,
`scripts/validate-registry.py`.
